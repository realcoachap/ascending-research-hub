The principle

The hub should feel like a professional research directory: tools, education, verification, and public data each have a clear purpose. The separation is real because the wording, UX, and links keep each lane disciplined.

Ask AI laneTheo handles general literacy, mechanisms, terms, and research context. No buying pressure.
Calculator laneArithmetic-only outputs: concentration, unit mapping, reverse volume math.
No commerce laneAscending Research does not host cart, account, checkout, or store preview surfaces. Ascending Aminos remains a separate property.
Trust architecture

Not a directory. A decision surface.

PeptideCritic sets a useful transparency bar with pricing, vendor data, COAs, and reviews. Ascending should feel more premium: education first, tools second, verification third, and public data without cart or account routing.

Separation by designEducation, tools, verification, and data each have a disciplined lane.
Verification-forwardCOA checks are treated as a primary navigation item, not fine print.
Premium restraintCleaner copy, fewer claims, stronger visual hierarchy, more confidence.
