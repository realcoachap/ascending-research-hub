Verify before you trust.

Use independent lab portals to check public COAs, certificate IDs, accession numbers, company names, and batch documentation. This page is a transparency directory, not an endorsement of any product, vendor, lab, or research compound.

What to match

  • Compound name and labeled amount
  • Batch, lot, certificate, or accession ID
  • Test date and issuing laboratory
  • Purity and identity methods such as HPLC and LC-MS
