Source 1:
Title: A Synthetic ERR Agonist Alleviates Metabolic Syndrome
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37739806/
Platform: PubMed
Tier: peer_reviewed
Topic: slu-pp-332

Plain-English source notes:
SLU-PP-332 is described as an agonist for estrogen-related receptor alpha, beta, and gamma nuclear receptors. The paper frames pharmacological exercise mimetics as potential tools for studying obesity and metabolic syndrome. In mouse models, SLU-PP-332 was reported to mimic some exercise-induced metabolic effects, including increased energy expenditure and fatty acid oxidation, accompanied by decreased fat mass accumulation and improved insulin sensitivity in models of metabolic syndrome. This is preclinical mouse-model evidence, not human-use guidance.
