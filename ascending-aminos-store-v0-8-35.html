FAQ

Clear answers before access review.

The public experience should answer the obvious questions quickly without drifting into protocols, dosing, or medical language.

Who can request review?

Qualified research professionals or organizations that complete the required age, research-use, and accuracy attestations.

Does Ascending Aminos provide medical guidance?

No. Product pages, account flows, and review requests are limited to research-use information and do not provide diagnosis, treatment, dosing, cycles, or protocols.

How should COA and lot data be checked?

Use the batch verifier and compare the lot, product name, release status, method, lab, test date, and COA record before accepting any research material.

Can guests browse?

Yes. Guest access can browse and verify lots, but requesting review requires a registered research account.
